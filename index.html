04 A position

The browser is
not a dumb
canvas.

We built abstraction upon abstraction until a document needed an operating system to say hello.

This is not nostalgia. Modern CSS has variables, logic, state, queries, animation, color systems, layout engines, and math. HTML has disclosure, validation, navigation, media, semantics, and accessibility baked in.

The constraint is the point. It forces the design to become clearer, the structure to become honest, and every byte to earn its passage across the wire.

“Start with what the platform gives you. You may be surprised how far it goes.”
